Testing on Ourselves First

4 min read · EMET Lab

Placeholder article, written to test the layout. The final text will replace it.

A living lab is a simple idea: before we recommend a way of working with AI agents, we work that way ourselves.

Our own agent sits in our team chat. It keeps track of our tasks, drafts with us, and tells us when something has stalled. When it gets something wrong, we see it first, and we learn from it first.

If a practice does not hold up inside our own team, it does not leave the lab.

What we watch for

We pay attention to the small moments: when the agent asks instead of guessing, when it says it is unsure, when it notices something a person missed, and when it oversteps. Each of these becomes a note, and the notes become method.

Why in the open

We publish our working notes as we go, so others can build on them or argue with them.
