What the Golem Teaches Us About AI Agents

6 min read · EMET Lab

Placeholder article, written to test the layout. The final text will replace it.

Long before anyone wrote a line of code, people told stories about making a helper. The golem is one of the oldest: a figure shaped from clay, brought to life by a single word, built to protect and to serve.

What makes the legend last is not the magic. It is the failure. The golem was strong and tireless, and it did exactly what it was told. It could not tell what its makers meant from what they said, and it could not notice when the situation had changed.

Capability without understanding rarely fails loudly. It fails obediently.

The same gap, a new material

AI agents are now asked to act, not only to answer. They run processes, draft decisions and speak with people. The gap the legend describes is the gap we meet every day: an instruction followed perfectly, in a situation it no longer fits.

Three lessons we carry

Context matters more than commands. Truth is what brings the work to life. And the people who make the helper stay responsible for it, with the power to stop it always in human hands.
