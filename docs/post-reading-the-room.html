Reading the Room: What Context Means for an Agent

7 min read · EMET Lab

Placeholder article, written to test the layout. The final text will replace it.

When people say an agent “missed the context”, they rarely mean one thing. They mean it missed who was asking, what had happened before, what was at stake, or what was left unsaid.

The same sentence can be a request, a complaint or a test. The words alone do not tell you which.

Four layers we are mapping

We are working with four layers: the person (who is asking, and how they are today), the history (what came before), the stakes (what happens if this goes wrong) and the setting (where, and in front of whom).

What we do not know yet

How much of this an agent should infer, and how much it should ask, is still open. That question drives most of our current work.
