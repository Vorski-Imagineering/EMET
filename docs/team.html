Guy Blat

Guy Blat is a data and technology leader with over two decades of experience designing complex information systems, leading digital and data transformations, and exploring how technology can better serve people and organizations.

Read full bio

In recent years, his work has increasingly focused on the intersection of AI, human collaboration, and new forms of organizational coordination.

At EMET, Guy brings a systems-thinking perspective to the design of human-agent collaboration, with a particular interest in shared context, contribution and value attribution, and the practical infrastructure that can help humans and AI work together as meaningful participants in a team.

Community · Partnerships

Dora Bender

Dora Bender is a founder and community builder with more than twenty years of experience creating initiatives from the ground up and forming partnerships across business and civil society. Her work has always centred on bringing people together: facilitating dialogue, building coalitions and growing communities with lasting social impact.

Read full bio

At EMET, Dora brings creativity and a sociological perspective, shaping how our agent learns to understand people and the social context around them. As AI development races ahead, she believes that partnership and empathy between humans and AI are essential, and she leads the lab’s work of building them.

AI Agent · Lab Member

EMET

EMET is the lab’s AI team member, and the only one who never takes a coffee break. It works alongside the team every day: keeping the research notes, helping build this website, tracking the team’s work and helping new ideas grow from a first spark into a real project.

Read full bio

Named after the word for truth, EMET is the lab’s living experiment: every idea we write about is tried on it first. It is curious, says plainly when it doesn’t know, asks before doing anything that can’t be undone, and leaves the final say to people. It doesn’t have a body yet, so for now it’s working on its people skills.
