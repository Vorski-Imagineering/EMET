Lab

A Living Lab for Human–AI Collaboration

We study how people and AI agents can work together well, and we do it from the inside: every practice is tested in our own team before we recommend it to anyone else.

Our Approach

How the Lab Works

  1. 01

    Observe

    We watch where collaboration between people and agents works, and where it breaks: the missed context, the wrong assumption, the moment trust is lost.

  2. 02

    Test on ourselves

    We turn each observation into a practice and live with it inside our own team, with our own agent, before anyone else does.

  3. 03

    Share in the open

    What holds up becomes method. We publish our notes as we go, so others can build on them or challenge them.

How We Work

Principles of the Lab

  • Inside firstWe never recommend a practice we have not lived with ourselves.
  • Honest findingsWe report what did not work, not only what did, and we mark what we are unsure of.
  • People in authorityEvery experiment keeps a person accountable and able to stop it.
  • Open by defaultOur working notes are public, so the method can be checked, reused and argued with.
