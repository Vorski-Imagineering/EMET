# EMET

**A consultancy practising empathic agent design.**

*Emet* (אמת) is the word inscribed on the golem's forehead, which animates the
made thing. Strike the aleph and *met* (מת) — "dead" — remains. It also means
**truth**.

Both readings are the standard of the practice. We build agents that are alive
to the people they serve, and we are honest about what they are and are not.

---

## What this repository is

The working surface of the practice, accumulated in public: how to design
agents that read context well, respond to people as people, and fail
gracefully. Not a product, and not a portfolio — an honest, append-only trail
of method as it develops.

Everything here is public and permanent.

## What is not here

No client material. No engagements, names, rates, proposals, or anything
learned inside an engagement — in any form, however abstracted. That boundary
is written down in [`AGENT.md`](./AGENT.md) §2.2 and is not a matter of
judgement.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `research/notes/` | Working notes — raw thinking, sub-questions, fragments |
| `research/synthesis/` | Clean, structured outputs that stand alone |
| `research/logs/` | Date-stamped activity logs — what was done, when, why |
| `specs/` | Design specs. Internal; not published |
| `docs/` | **The live website** at [emet.audax.earth](https://emet.audax.earth) |

Anything committed to `docs/` appears on the public web. Nothing internal goes
there.

## For agents

Read [`AGENT.md`](./AGENT.md) before doing anything. It is the protocol:
confidentiality policy, naming conventions, the required workflow loop,
citation format, and the stop-and-ask triggers. `CLAUDE.md` is a symlink to it;
`AGENTS.md` is the same document with the Codex memory path.
